Chopper Stabilization Modulated Zero-Drift Amplification

Analog & AFE

Technique using dynamic switch matrices to modulate DC microvolt signals up to high frequency (\(f_{\text{ch}} = 250\,\text{kHz}\)) prior to amplification. Demodulation restores the baseband signal while up-modulating amplifier native \(V_{os}\) and 1/f flicker noise to high frequency for removal by the Sinc³ filter.

Delta-Sigma ADC (ΔΣ) 2nd-Order Switched-Capacitor Oversampling Converter

ADC architecture utilizing oversampling and high-order noise shaping. A 2nd-order modulator imposes a \((1 - z^{-1})^2\) noise transfer function, suppressing baseband quantization noise to achieve SNR > 98 dB (ENOB > 16.0 bits) at OSR = 256.

AGC Hardware Automatic Gain Control

Engine covering a 5-decade current dynamic range (15 mA sleep current up to 1500A starter short-circuit). Fast hardware comparators monitor modulator output and dynamically switch PGA gain across 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x, 32x, and 64x in a single sample cycle.

Sinc³ Filter Third-Order Decimation Comb Filter

Analog & AFE

Digital hardware decimation filter consisting of 3 integrator stages and 3 differentiator stages: \(H(z) = (\frac{1 - z^{-D}}{1 - z^{-1}})^3\). Naturally places deep transmission zeros (notches) at the chopping frequency and its harmonics to eradicate ripples.

Shunt Wire-Break Active Micro-Current Injection Diagnostic Circuit

Functional Safety

Hardware diagnostic mechanism. Periodically injects 50 nA into ISENSE_P and sinks 50 nA from ISENSE_N. An intact 100 µΩ shunt incurs only 5 pV perturbation; a fractured solder joint immediately charges pins to 3.3V rail saturation, firing an NMI in < 2 µs.

Hazard Prevention:Prevents floating input pins from picking up EMI that mimics normal discharge current Jump to Chapter 06 · Wire-Break Diagnostic Schematic →

Challenge-Response WDT 16-Bit LFSR Window Watchdog

Hardware watchdog driven by an independent low-speed oscillator (ILO). Generates pseudo-random questions using a 16-bit LFSR polynomial (\(x^{16} + x^{14} + x^{13} + x^{11} + 1\)). CPU must execute self-tests and return the correct polynomial token within a valid time window.

Protection:Early or late servicing triggers instant hardware reset; detects runaway CPU stuck in loops Jump to Chapter 06 · LFSR Watchdog Schematic →
