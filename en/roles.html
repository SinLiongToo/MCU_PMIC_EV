2. Automotive High-Frequency Interview Prep

Q1: Why are Sigma-Delta ADCs preferred over SAR ADCs for automotive BMS current sensing?
Key points: Dynamic range (> 100 dB to cover both 15mA sleep and 1500A cranking across a 100 µΩ shunt) and intrinsic noise filtering (Sinc decimation filter places transmission notches at 50/60 Hz and PWM inverter harmonics).
Q2: What is the key difference between a standard Window Watchdog and a Challenge-Response Watchdog?
Key points: A standard window watchdog only enforces feeding timing. A challenge-response watchdog also tests algorithmic logic: the MCU must execute safety self-tests and calculate a dynamic token from a pseudo-random seed, preventing execution loops from faking health.
