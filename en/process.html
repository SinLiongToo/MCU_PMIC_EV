Chapter 04 · High-Voltage Process & Silicon Integration

High-Voltage Silicon Process: Harmonizing Microvolts and 42V on a Single Die

Integrating 1.8V dense logic, microvolt-sensitive differential analog amplifiers, and 42V high-voltage power switches on one monolithic piece of silicon requires BCD (Bipolar-CMOS-DMOS) smart power fabrication technology.

1. BCD Smart Power Fabrication

  • Bipolar (BJT): Ultra-low 1/f flicker noise and precise temperature linearity for the on-chip bandgap reference and low-noise operational preamplifiers.
  • CMOS: High gate density and minimal static leakage for the 48 MHz Cortex-M0+ core, 128KB Flash, and digital decimation filters.
  • DMOS (LDMOS): Lateral double-diffused MOSFETs with breakdown voltage \(V_{BR} > 45\text{ V}\) for the internal 42V regulator and LIN driver.

2. Deep Trench Isolation (DTI) & Substrate Noise Immunity

High-voltage switching injects minority carriers into the p-substrate. Deep Trench Isolation (DTI) creates vertical silicon dioxide and polysilicon insulating barriers down into the buried oxide, attenuating substrate noise by over 40 dB and preventing CMOS latch-up.

3. ISO 7637-2 Load Dump Pulse 5b Transient Clamping

Active dynamic clamp circuits turn on large-area DMOS power structures when VBAT surges past 38V, safely absorbing surge energy and preventing thermal second breakdown.

4. Automotive Thermal Management (-40°C to +125°C / +150°C)

Qualified to AEC-Q100 Grade 1, PSoC™ 4 HVPA operates reliably in engine compartments up to +125°C ambient and +150°C junction temperature with a thermal resistance \(\theta_{JC} < 2.5\text{ °C/W}\) through its bottom exposed pad.
