Chapter 05 · Test, Calibration & Automotive Qualification

Testing, Trimming & AEC-Q100: Industrial Journey to Zero Defects

Consumer silicon allows occasional RMA returns; automotive microcontrollers mandate Zero PPM (parts per million). A single errant voltage sample during sub-zero highway driving could trip high-voltage contactors and stall the powertrain. This chapter examines wafer probe testing, eFuse trimming, and AEC-Q100 qualification.

1. Wafer Probe (CP) & eFuse Precision Analog Trimming

\[V_{\text{corrected}} = \text{Gain\_Trim} \times (V_{\text{raw}} - \text{Offset\_Trim}) + T_{\text{coeff}} \times (T - T_0)\] On-chip non-volatile eFuses store laser/ATE-measured calibration coefficients, trimming bandgap references to ±0.05% and reducing raw ADC offset from ±250 µV down to sub-microvolt levels.

2. Tri-Temp Production Screening (-40°C, +25°C, +125°C)

Cold test (-40°C) screens for timing race conditions and threshold shifts; hot test (+125°C ~ +150°C) weeds out oxide tunneling, high-temperature gate leakage, and early life bathtub curve failures.

3. AEC-Q100 Qualification Protocols

HTOL (1000 hours at 125°C, zero fails across 231 units), HAST (130°C / 85% RH bias), temperature cycling (-55°C to +150°C 1000 cycles), and electrostatic discharge (ESD ≥ 2kV HBM, ≥ 8kV LIN).

4. Wettable Flank QFN Packaging for 100% AOI Optical Inspection

Step-cut plated flanks allow solder to wick up the sidewall of the package, forming visible solder fillets that enable high-speed Automated Optical Inspection (AOI) cameras on automotive surface-mount lines.
