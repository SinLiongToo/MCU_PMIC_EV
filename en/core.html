Core Microarchitecture: Computation, Bus & Hardware Safety Circuits

In automotive battery management systems (BMS), microcontrollers must balance ultra-low standby quiescent power with deterministic, hard real-time interrupt responsiveness. Taking the Infineon PSoC™ 4 HVPA-144K as a baseline, this chapter covers the Arm® Cortex®-M0+ core, the single-cycle I/O concept with an external drive circuit, the 8-channel DMA, SECDED ECC logic, and the power modes with datasheet current figures.

Device figures: Infineon PSoC™ 4 HVPA-144K datasheet 002-28660 Rev. *K (“DS”). Core figures: Arm Cortex-M0+ Technical Reference Manual.

1. Single-Cycle Fast I/O Bus & Level Shifter Pyro-Fuse Drive Circuit

When legacy microcontrollers manipulate GPIO pins via CPU instructions, access must pass through standard system buses (AHB/APB) and peripheral bridge arbiters, incurring 2 to 4 clock cycles of latency. In battery short-circuit events, actuating a Pyro-fuse (pyrotechnic circuit breaker) must occur within tens of microseconds. The Cortex-M0+ architecture offers an optional single-cycle I/O port (IOPORT) that lets the core reach GPIO registers without AHB arbitration. Whether a device implements it, and where it is mapped, is the silicon vendor’s choice — check the device TRM.

Note: HVPA GPIOs run from the 3.3 V VDDD rail (DS: up to 9 GPIOs, ±20 mA per pin). The 12 V firing loop on the right is an external gate driver and MOSFET example, not part of the HVPA. Real disconnect time is dominated by fault detection, the driver and the pyro device itself (milliseconds); single-cycle I/O saves only tens of nanoseconds.

2. SRAM SECDED Hamming Code Hardware XOR Error-Correction Architecture

High-energy atmospheric neutron flux in automotive environments can induce Single Event Upsets (SEU), unpredictably flipping SRAM bits. PSoC™ 4 HVPA protects SRAM, code flash and data flash with single-error-correct, double-error-detect (SECDED) ECC (DS §1.1.2). Single-bit errors are corrected in-line and the error address and data are logged; uncorrectable errors go to the fault system, which raises an interrupt or reset as configured.

4. AHB-Lite Interconnect & 8-Channel DMA

The current and voltage channels convert continuously at kSPS rates. Interrupting the CPU for every result would waste most of its time on interrupt entry and exit. The HVPA DMA has 8 channels with 32-bit transfers and chainable ping-pong descriptors, and it keeps working while the CPU sleeps (DS §1.1.6).
