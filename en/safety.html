Functional Safety & Reliability: ISO 26262 ASIL-C in Practice

In automotive battery disconnect units and high-voltage packs, an undetected runaway current or an accidental contactor drop-out poses severe thermal and vehicular safety hazards. Grounded in ISO 26262, the PSoC™ 4 HVPA natively achieves ASIL-C certification.

1. Automotive Safety Integrity Levels (ASIL) & Safety Goals

Core safety goals include preventing battery thermal runaway from sustained overcurrent (ASIL-C/D) and preventing false disconnection during highway cruising (ASIL-C).

2. Five On-Chip Hardware Safety Mechanisms

  • Challenge-Response Window Watchdog: Dual-layer protection enforcing strict open/close timing windows and requiring algorithmic calculation of dynamic challenge tokens from independent 32 kHz ILO clocks.
  • Memory SECDED ECC & Fault Injection: Hardware corrects single-bit flips and issues Non-Maskable Interrupts (NMI) on fatal double-bit faults.
  • Analog BIST & Wire-Break Detection: 50 nA test current pulses verify shunt solder continuity and detect open circuits immediately.
  • Voltage & Clock Supervisors: Brown-Out (BOD) and Over-Voltage Detect (OVD) monitoring all power rails.
